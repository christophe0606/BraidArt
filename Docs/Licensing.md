# Third-party license notices

The bundled third-party components and help illustration retain their own
licenses, copyright notices and source references. This document describes those
third-party materials only.

- [Complete inventory, license texts and source links](../Licenses/README.md)
- [Solver release details](ThirdParty.md)
- [Upstream change record](../CBC_CHANGES/README.md)

Both the EPL 2.0 release licenses and EPL 1.0 library licenses are preserved
verbatim, together with the author files, generated-parser exception, full Bison
GPL text, AMPL notices, and rational-approximation attribution. Each component's
`ORIGIN.md` records its exact upstream release, source archive URL and checksum.

The NASA photograph's public-domain attribution, original photograph link and
image-rights record are retained in the inventory and displayed in the app.

`Scripts/package-licenses.sh` bundles these third-party notices on macOS and iOS.
It does not bundle an application license. Upstream source files remain
unmodified; any future edits must be recorded with their original notices and
source-availability material as described in the change record.
